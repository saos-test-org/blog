## Why we show every change

Plenty of teams already use agents. The hard question is what those agents actually did, and who said yes. On the showcase site you can check that for yourself, one change at a time.

SAOS turns company knowledge into explicit, versioned records. People and agents work from that same source of truth. Each agent has a defined identity and authority, and it leaves evidence of what it did.
